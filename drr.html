Sendai Framework & Living Heritage

The ICH Disaster Resilience Radar operationalizes the Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction 2015–2030 (Priority 1 & Priority 4) and the 2020 UNESCO Operational Principles for Safeguarding ICH in Emergencies.

Priority 1: Understanding Risk

Indigenous warning lore (e.g., Smong, Pranata Mangsa) provides hyper-localized hazard predictive capacity.

Priority 2: Strengthening Governance

Customary water and fire institutions (Subak, Valencia Water Court) govern resource crises peacefully.

Priority 3: Resilience Investing

Vernacular architecture (Kigumi, Ivatan) provides passive, zero-energy structural resilience.

Priority 4: Build Back Better

Traditional mutual-aid and wetland systems (Baira, Gotong Royong) ensure rapid community post-disaster food recovery.
